# Groundwork — Growth Roadmap

Living to-do list for expanding beyond cold email/SMS outreach. Read this at
the start of any session touching growth/monetization work — update it at
the end of one instead of letting decisions live only in chat history.

## The core thesis (2026-07-24)

Cold email/SMS mostly reaches businesses that already have a website and
already have their online presence "sorted" — low urgency, low conversion.
The ~5% magic-link click rate we DO see is the real signal: people are
willing to look. The job now is reaching people who feel the actual
*necessity* of a website — no_website/no-presence trades businesses, and
established businesses who'd genuinely feel "£24.99/mo is a steal" once
they see the quality of what we build them. Sales come from necessity, not
from a good pitch to someone who doesn't feel the gap. Two levers:

1. **Reach more people who feel the necessity** (no-website segment,
   social-native trades audiences) — this is what workstreams 1-2 below are
   about.
2. **Make the thing itself so good the necessity becomes obvious** even
   with thin source data (a Facebook-only or no-website prospect has far
   less to personalize from than a business with an existing site) — this
   is workstream 4.

## Workstream 1 — Facebook DM outreach (SHIPPED 2026-07-24)

Manual queue at `/admin/facebook-outreach`. See
`docs/outreach-pipeline-spec.md` Section 20 for the full design. Captures
`facebook_page_url` from the existing nightly email-discovery search
(routine updated 2026-07-24 to include it), logs sends as
`OutreachTouch(channel="facebook")`.

**Deliberately manual** — Meta's Messenger Platform requires the recipient
to message first, always; there is no compliant API path for a business to
send the first message to someone who hasn't contacted them. Confirmed via
research 2026-07-24 (see Workstream 2 below for the one real exception).

**Not yet done**: a follow-up drip for this channel (currently single
initial-touch only).

## Workstream 2 — Social presence + comment-to-DM automation (NOT STARTED)

Idea: build a real content presence (not just ads) on platforms trades
audiences actually engage with, with posts designed to earn comments, then
auto-reply to commenters via Messenger's **Private Reply** API (Meta
Send API) with a personalized magic link — this part CAN be fully
automated and is compliant, because the commenter's comment is what
initiates contact, not us.

**Confirmed via research 2026-07-24:**
- Private Reply: one automated reply per comment/post, must fire within
  7 days of the comment (Live broadcasts: bounded to the broadcast). This
  is the compliant automation path — no human needs to click send for
  *this* reply, since it's responding to inbound engagement, not cold
  outreach.
- This does NOT let us target our existing sourced prospect list by
  name — only people who actually see and comment on our own content
  become reachable. It's a parallel top-of-funnel acquisition channel,
  not a replacement for Workstream 1's targeted queue.
- Realistic reach requires paid ad spend behind posts (organic reach for
  a new/small Page is low) — "post and hope" alone won't surface the
  right audience at volume.

**What "fully automate posts" actually means, split by what's compliant:**
- **Publishing posts/content itself** — fully automatable, no platform
  restriction (this is just publishing your own content to your own Page,
  same as Buffer/Hootsuite do it). Meta Graph API (Facebook Pages),
  Instagram Content Publishing API, TikTok Content Posting API all
  support this legitimately.
- **Auto-replying to a comment with a magic link** — fully automatable via
  Private Reply, per above, since it's response-to-inbound not
  cold-outbound.
- **Designing what to post** — the interesting optimization problem. Same
  shape as the email-variant-testing system already built
  (`outreach/variant_optimizer_job.py`) — generate candidates, test,
  measure engagement, promote winners, same evidence-library-driven
  approach as `docs/cold-email-evidence-library.md`. Claude Code doing the
  actual post copy/design generation (mirroring how email-variant
  candidates are already generated by a scheduled routine) is a
  reasonable, buildable approach — this is genuinely an optimization job,
  not a one-off design task.

**Platform recommendation** (initial view, worth revisiting once we have
real data): **Instagram + Facebook first** (visual before/after
site-preview content, behind-the-scenes generation content — construction
trades audiences are already there, and Meta's ecosystem is what the
Private Reply automation lives in anyway, so the DM-capture loop stays in
one platform family). **TikTok second** — real potential for the "spreads
like wildfire among tradesmen" instinct (trade-content and
before/after/reveal-style videos do genuinely well on TikTok's discovery
algorithm, and TikTok skews toward exactly the kind of organic virality a
referral-driven trades product wants), but no comment-to-DM equivalent
automation exists there — would need its own lead-capture mechanism
(bio link, TikTok's own lead-gen ad units). **LinkedIn**: deprioritize —
audience skews corporate/office rather than tools-on-a-van trades, weakest
fit of the options.

**Not yet scoped**: which platform(s) to build first, what the actual post
content/cadence looks like, ad budget, and whether to build the
posting/engagement-tracking pipeline in this repo or via an external tool.

## Workstream 3 — Affiliate/referral revenue (IN PROGRESS)

**Done (2026-07-24)**: `privacy.html` / `terms.html` published (prerequisite
for any affiliate application) — both linked from every public page's
footer. Awin identified as the fastest path to Tide/Starling/Simply
Business/Xero-type B2B referral programs under one application.

**Not yet done**: actual Awin publisher signup (needs the user's own
business/banking details — not something I can do on their behalf),
per-advertiser applications once approved, building the actual placement
(loading page / preview page slot).

**New idea from this session, not yet scoped**: drill into what
established-business prospects who click their preview link are actually
looking for beyond the website itself — i.e. segment "already has an
organized online presence" clickers specifically, since they're a
different opportunity than the no-website segment (their gap isn't "I
need a website," it might be "I need X other thing," where affiliate
products could be the actual conversion lever rather than the website
subscription itself). No research done yet on what that "X" is — this
needs its own investigation pass (could be interviews, could be inferring
from what services those businesses already have/lack).

## Workstream 4 — Client-as-affiliate program (IDEA, NOT STARTED)

Once there's a real paying customer base: give customers a referral
incentive (cash or credit) for bringing in other tradespeople. Rationale:
construction/trades is a genuinely social, word-of-mouth-driven industry —
a good product with a financial incentive attached could spread through
trade networks in a way generic ads can't. Needs: a customer base to draw
from first (chicken-and-egg with current conversion rate), a referral
tracking mechanism (unique referral codes/links per customer, likely
reusing the same link-identity/token infrastructure the outreach pipeline
already has), and a payout mechanism (Stripe Connect or manual payout —
not yet decided).

## Workstream 5 — Generation quality for thin-data prospects (ONGOING)

A Facebook-only or no-website prospect has far less real material to
personalize from than a business with an existing site to extract
logo/photos/copy from. Several fixes shipped 2026-07-24 already push
generation quality up broadly (mobile-responsive requirements, banned
fabricated placeholder/testimonial text, first-person brand voice for the
no-quotes-reviews case) — but this is a standing concern, not a one-time
fix: the thinner the source data, the more the generation quality has to
carry the "this is worth £24.99/mo" impression on its own. Worth
revisiting specifically through the lens of "what does our best possible
output look like when all we have is a business name, trade, and
location" — no dedicated pass has been done from that specific angle yet.

## Context worth remembering

- Email/SMS conversion is intentionally not the growth lever right now —
  that channel mostly reaches businesses that already feel no urgency.
  This isn't a failure to fix, it's a structurally low-intent channel.
- The ~5% magic-link click rate is the number to protect/grow — it's
  evidence real demand exists once the necessity is felt.
- "Sales come from necessity" — every workstream above should be judged by
  whether it reaches people who'd feel that necessity, not just by reach
  or engagement numbers in isolation.
