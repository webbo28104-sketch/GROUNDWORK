Questions

Straight answers

Do I pay anything to see my website?

No. You see the real, finished website free, watermarked as a preview. You only pay if you decide to go live.

What's the £99 actually for?

It's a one-off fee for building your site and publishing it live on your own address. It's charged once, at the point you choose to go live — never before.

What web address will my site have?

Your site goes live on a groundworkbuild.com address as soon as you're ready. Support for connecting your own existing domain is on the way and will be available very soon!

Can I edit my site?

You can edit any of the text before you go live. Need something changed after that? Just email us and we'll sort it.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly billing is £24.99 with no minimum term — cancel any time. The annual option is a single upfront payment for the year, taken in exchange for the lower rate.

Is there a cheaper way to pay?

Yes — pay annually at £249.99/year instead of £24.99 a month (£299.88 across a year). That's a saving of £49.89, around 17%, billed once upfront rather than monthly.

Will pricing change?

Current pricing is £99 for setup and £24.99 a month, and it may increase as demand grows. If you're already subscribed, your rate is locked in and won't change for as long as you stay subscribed. If you cancel and later resubscribe, the pricing in effect at that time would apply.

Is the first month really free?

Yes — on the monthly plan, you pay the £99 setup fee to go live, and your first month of hosting is free. Your card is billed £24.99 automatically from the second month onward, unless you cancel first.

What if I don't like it?

Then you simply don't pay — nothing goes live and nothing's owed. There's no obligation at the preview stage.
