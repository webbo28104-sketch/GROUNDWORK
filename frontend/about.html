About

We take the admin out of getting a website.

Built for trades businesses, at a fraction of the usual cost.

Groundwork started with a simple observation: trades businesses do brilliant work, but too many are stuck without a proper website to show for it. Not because they don't want one — because getting one is a hassle. Agencies are expensive and slow. DIY builders eat a weekend you don't have. So the website gets put off, indefinitely.

We built Groundwork to remove that admin entirely. Tell us about your business, and a professional website is generated around your services, your area and your own logo colours — ready to look at free, in a couple of minutes. No back-and-forth with a designer, no wrestling with a website builder, no waiting weeks.

Trades businesses are judged on trust and presentation as much as any business, often more. A website shouldn't be the reason a good business looks less credible than a worse one with a slicker site. Groundwork exists to close that gap — properly done, at a fraction of the usual cost.

The point of it

If you do good work, you shouldn't lose the job to someone with worse skills and a better website. We take away the admin of getting one — quickly, honestly, and for a fraction of the cost.
