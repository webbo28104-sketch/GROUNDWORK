Why it matters

The real difference a website makes

Before a customer picks up the phone, many will look you up. What they find — or don't find — shapes the decision before you've said a word.

Without a website
  • Nothing comes up when a customer searches for your business
  • No way to show your accreditations or past work before they call
  • Looks less established than competitors who do have a site
  • Enquiries land on a bare social media page instead of a proper site
With a Groundwork site
  • A professional website with your business name on it
  • One place to send every enquiry, with your work on display
  • Presents your business on equal footing with any competitor
  • Puts you in contention for larger, higher-value work
